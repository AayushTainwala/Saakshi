# Saakshi

A free, open-source app for iPhone and Android that helps people in India protect themselves and their rights: record evidence fit for court under the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, alert your trusted contacts with your location, protect yourself when your phone is taken, and know what to do, in English and Hindi.

Published by Aayush Tainwala, Advocate.

**Status:** in development. Nothing is released yet.
